Study & Relaxation TipsScience-backed strategies for student wellness
📚
Study Smarter
  • Use the Pomodoro Technique (25min work + 5min break)
  • Active recall beats passive re-reading every time
  • Study in well-lit, distraction-free environments
  • Teach concepts to others to solidify understanding
  • Review notes within 24 hours of learning them
  • Use spaced repetition for long-term retention
🧘
Stress Relief
  • Practice 5 minutes of deep breathing daily
  • Progressive muscle relaxation before bed
  • Journaling helps process complex emotions
  • Limit social media during stressful periods
  • Cold water on your face activates calm reflex
  • The 5-4-3-2-1 grounding technique for anxiety
😴
Better Sleep
  • Aim for 7-9 hours of quality sleep each night
  • Keep a consistent sleep-wake schedule
  • Avoid screens 1 hour before bedtime
  • Keep your room cool and dark for better sleep
  • Avoid caffeine after 2 PM
  • A short 20-min nap can boost alertness
🏃
Physical Wellness
  • 30 minutes of exercise boosts mood significantly
  • Stay hydrated — dehydration causes brain fog
  • Eat brain foods: nuts, berries, fish, leafy greens
  • Stretch every 45 minutes when studying
  • Morning sunlight regulates your body clock
  • Walk 10 mins after meals to aid focus
